# Credits

All art and sound in this mod were generated for it with fal (`um fal`):
- pixel-art damage numbers (a generated digit strip), hearts, flames, stars, blood splat and slime faces: fal image models (Nano Banana 2 / GPT Image 2).
- sound effects and the theme music: ElevenLabs sound-effects and music models through fal.

Portal 2 models, sounds and particles are used from the installed game (cubes, personality spheres, crown, portals). No assets were taken from Terraria or any other game.
